- Use design tokens only: primary #0F6CBD, success #067647, warning #B54708, destructive #B42318.
- UI is light-first, professional and operational; no HUD or sci-fi treatment.
- Every state has text/icon as well as color. Interactive targets are at least 44 px.
- Preserve keyboard, focus, labelled landmark and EN/ES resource parity.
- Motion is 150–200 ms maximum and respects reduced motion.
